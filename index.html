Smart Silent Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. About Smart Silent

Smart Silent is a mobile application designed to help users manage phone sound profiles and schedules, including Silent, Vibrate and Normal modes.

2. Information We Collect

Smart Silent may use information and permissions necessary to provide its functionality. We do not intend to collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address unless you voluntarily provide it.

3. Permissions

The app may request certain Android permissions necessary for its functionality, including notification, device sound settings and scheduling-related permissions where supported by Android.

4. Data Storage

Information and settings created within the app may be stored locally on your device to support app functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information is not shared except where necessary to operate the app, comply with applicable laws or protect our rights.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Smart Silent is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Privacy

You may stop using the app and uninstall it at any time. Locally stored app data may be removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to Android device behavior.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
